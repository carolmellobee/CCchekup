    Spending
    
        
    Purchases only. Payments, refunds and reversals are excluded from totals and listed separately, because counting them would flatter your numbers.
    
      
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    
        
    Forecast
    
        
    Four models compete on your own history. Each is scored by walk-forward backtesting, and the ensemble weights them by how wrong they were.

    Rules & data
    
        
    Categorisation is keyword matching, top to bottom, first match wins. It is not clever and it does not need to be.
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Category keywords
    
        
    One category per line: Category = keyword, keyword, keyword
    
        
        
    
          
          
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Custom line pattern
    
        
    If the built-in patterns miss your issuer, write a regular expression with three capture groups, in order: date, description, amount. Applied after the built-ins fail.
